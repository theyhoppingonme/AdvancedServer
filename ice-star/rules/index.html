Here are the basic rules of the server, breaking this rules will get you banned:

  • No cheating, this include Sonic MOD new jump system, spindash, dropped rings collection. However Tails' flying system from "Advanced Client" mod is allowed.
  • No exploiting/hacking, this also includes chat spamming, avoiding votekick by rejoining, entity art scripts, ability delay mods. Custom Palettes are allowed.
A rule violation is still a violation — even if you're trying to retaliate against someone who broke the rules first. If you dislike another player's behavior, do not take matters into your own hands. Instead, ask an admin for help (if one is available), leave the server or switch lobbies, or start a vote to kick the player if no staff are present. When you're angry or upset, you're not in control of your actions — and that's when more rules get broken.

Soft rules and ethical recommendations

  • Don't camp bodies or constantly target the same player. This isn't a strict rule, but, please, avoid doing it. Using what kind of tactics can ruin the experience for others. Be mindful of how your actions affect the game. (You most likely won't be banned, but either kicked by the administrator, or vote-kicked from the server by others)
  • Don't harras anyone in the chat. Be aware, that there's no /mute mechanic like in minecraft, so instead you'll be kicked.
Long story short, use common sense and be respectful to others, this includes both. If unsure, ask an administrator or other players.
